⚠️ Safety First

Bring Enough Water

Carry at least 1 gallon per person per day. Dehydration is the leading cause of rescue operations.

Know Your Limits

Every trail into the canyon is steep. What goes down must come back up. Allow twice as much time for the return trip.

Check Weather

Summer temperatures at the river exceed 100°F. Winter brings ice and snow on upper trails. Always check conditions before hiking.
