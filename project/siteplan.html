Site Plan: Grand Canyon Adventures

Site Name

Grand Canyon Adventures

Reason for Selection: This name was chosen because it clearly identifies the subject (Grand Canyon) and the nature of the site (adventures/tourism). It is memorable, descriptive, and immediately tells visitors what to expect. The name appeals to outdoor enthusiasts, families planning vacations, and adventure seekers looking for information about visiting one of the world's most famous natural wonders.

Optional Domain: grandcanyonadventures.org

Site Purpose

The Grand Canyon Adventures website serves as a comprehensive guide for visitors planning a trip to the Grand Canyon. The site provides:

  • Detailed information about the best viewpoints, hiking trails, and scenic routes
  • Weather updates and seasonal recommendations for optimal visiting times
  • Accommodation and camping options both inside and near the park
  • Safety tips and preparation guides for hiking and outdoor activities
  • A photo gallery showcasing the canyon's beauty to inspire visitors
  • Contact information and links to official National Park Service resources

Scenarios

These questions represent the target audience (tourists, hikers, families, photographers) and drive the content strategy:

  • What are the best hiking trails for beginners, and what safety equipment should I bring?
  • Where can I find affordable lodging near the South Rim, and how far in advance should I book?
  • What is the best time of year to visit for photography, and which viewpoints offer sunrise views?

Color Usage:

  • Deep Blue (#1B4965): Used for all headings (h1, h2, h3), navigation backgrounds, footer borders, and primary buttons. Represents the Colorado River and sky.
  • Coral Orange (#FF6B35): Used for accent borders, call-to-action buttons, links on hover, and highlight boxes. Represents canyon rock formations and sunset colors.
  • Dark Slate (#2C3E50): Used for all body text and paragraph content. Ensures high contrast and readability.
  • Light Gray (#F8F9FA): Used for the page background to reduce eye strain and provide subtle contrast with content cards.

Typography

Two complementary fonts have been selected to create visual hierarchy and readability:

Merriweather (Serif) - Used for all headings (h1, h2, h3)

A classic, readable serif font that conveys trustworthiness and elegance. Applied to page titles and section headings.

Open Sans (Sans-Serif) - Used for body text, paragraphs, navigation, and UI elements

A clean, modern sans-serif font optimized for web readability. Applied to all body content, lists, and navigation menus.
