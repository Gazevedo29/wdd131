Why Visit the Grand Canyon?

🥾

World-Class Hiking

From easy rim walks to challenging descents, the Grand Canyon offers trails for every skill level. The Bright Angel Trail provides water and shade, while the South Kaibab offers stunning panoramic views.

📸

Breathtaking Photography

Capture sunrise at Mather Point, sunset at Hopi Point, or the Milky Way from this International Dark Sky Park. Every viewpoint tells a different story.

🏕️

Camping & Lodging

Stay inside the park at Bright Angel Lodge or camp under the stars. Book well in advance as accommodations fill up quickly year-round.

Current Weather & Seasonal Tips

South Rim Conditions

72°F Sunny
Elevation: 7,000 ft
Best Season: Spring/Fall

Seasonal Recommendations

Spring (March-May): Ideal weather with temperatures between 50-80°F. Wildflowers bloom along the rim. Water sources are reliable. Book lodging 6+ months ahead.
